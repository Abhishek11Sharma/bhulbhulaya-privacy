Privacy Policy

Bhul Bhulaya · Android app · Last updated 19 November 2026

In short: Bhul Bhulaya is an offline maze game. It collects no personal data and never connects to the internet.

Information we collect

None. Bhul Bhulaya has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Stars, best times, collected diyas, chosen theme and settings are saved only on your phone.
  • If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your own device backup, which Google manages under your Google account settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. You can also erase progress any time in Settings → Reset progress.

Permissions

  • Vibration – used only for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Children's privacy

Bhul Bhulaya is made with children in mind. It does not collect, use or disclose personal information from anyone, including children, in line with Google Play's Families policy and COPPA.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
